About Griddle

A whole site about one hot, flat surface.

Griddle is a small, independent cooking site about the most basic tool in the kitchen: a pan that gets hot.

Most recipes say “cook over medium-high heat” and leave the rest to you. That instruction hides most of what goes wrong at the stove. We explain heat, timing and pan care in plain language so that any recipe works better.

How we write

From everyday cooking on griddles, skillets and cast iron, checked against published food-safety guidance on cooking temperatures and reviewed by a second reader. Times and temperatures are averages; hobs and pans vary.

What we do not do

We do not sell cookware, rate brands or accept payment for mentions. We do not give dietary or medical advice.
